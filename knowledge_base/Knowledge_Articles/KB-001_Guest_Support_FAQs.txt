HOW DO I KNOW MY BOOKING IS CONFIRMED?
--------------------------------------
Check for a confirmation message containing a reservation ID and verify that the booking status is Confirmed. A payment notification alone does not prove confirmation.

I DID NOT RECEIVE A CONFIRMATION. WHAT SHOULD I DO?
---------------------------------------------------
Contact support with your reservation ID if available. If you do not have one, support will use the approved verification process to search for the booking.

CAN I CHANGE MY DATES OR ROOM?
------------------------------
Changes depend on the reservation terms, availability and property approval. Support will check the booking-specific conditions.

CAN I CANCEL AND RECEIVE A REFUND?
----------------------------------
Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation terms attached to your reservation and the recorded cancellation outcome. See the refund status guidance.

WHAT SHOULD I DO IF THE PROPERTY CANNOT FIND MY BOOKING?
--------------------------------------------------------
Contact support and provide your reservation ID. Do not create a second booking until support checks the existing reservation and payment status.

CAN SOMEONE ELSE CONTACT SUPPORT FOR MY BOOKING?
------------------------------------------------
Reservation details are shared only after the approved verification or authorisation process.

HOW DO I KNOW ONBOARDING IS COMPLETE?
-------------------------------------
Check the partner portal status or the formal activation notice. Uploading documents does not by itself mean the property is approved or live.

HOW DO I CORRECT A PROPERTY LISTING?
------------------------------------
Submit the correction through the authorised listing workflow and provide accurate supporting details where requested.

WHAT IF I CANNOT FIND A RESERVATION?
------------------------------------
Search by the reservation ID and verify property mapping, dates and partner account. If a confirmed reservation remains missing, contact partner support.

CAN I CANCEL A CONFIRMED RESERVATION DIRECTLY?
----------------------------------------------
Use the authorised platform process and provide a permitted reason. Do not bypass the platform or make an informal promise about the guest's refund.

WHERE CAN I CHECK SETTLEMENT DETAILS?
-------------------------------------
Use the partner settlement statement and reference period. For a discrepancy, contact finance support with the statement and reservation references.

HOW DO I CHANGE PAYOUT DETAILS?
-------------------------------
Use the secure verified account-change workflow. Support will not request passwords, OTPs or full banking credentials.

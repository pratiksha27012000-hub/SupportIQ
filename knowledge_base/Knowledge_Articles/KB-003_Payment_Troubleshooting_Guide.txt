PAYMENT SAYS PENDING
--------------------
A pending or authorised payment may not be a completed capture. Check the provider/ledger status and reservation status separately.

MONEY APPEARS DEBITED BUT NO BOOKING CONFIRMATION ARRIVED
---------------------------------------------------------
Do not assume the booking is confirmed. Contact support with a transaction reference and approximate timestamp; support will check the reservation and payment records.

PAYMENT FAILED
--------------
Check the provider status and use the approved retry guidance. Avoid repeated attempts until the status of any earlier attempt is understood.

I SEE TWO CHARGES
-----------------
Share the relevant transaction references through the approved secure support channel. Support will determine whether both are captured transactions or one is pending.

WHAT DETAILS SHOULD I NEVER SHARE?
----------------------------------
Never share your password, PIN, OTP, CVV or full payment-card number with support.

REFUND IS NOT VISIBLE
---------------------
Ask support to verify whether a refund was eligible, initiated, processing or completed. Any timeline must be based on the recorded payment-provider estimate.

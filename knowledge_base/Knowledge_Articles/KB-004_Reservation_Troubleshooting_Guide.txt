BOOKING STATUS IS PENDING
-------------------------
A pending request is not a confirmed reservation. Wait for the official status update or contact support if it remains unresolved.

CONFIRMATION EMAIL/MESSAGE IS MISSING
-------------------------------------
Search your registered email/app notifications and contact support with your reservation ID or the details requested for verification.

PROPERTY CANNOT LOCATE MY RESERVATION
-------------------------------------
Provide the reservation ID and stay dates to support. The team will compare the StayEase record with the property mapping.

NAME OR DATE IS INCORRECT
-------------------------
Contact support. A correction or modification is not complete until the authoritative reservation record is updated.

I MAY HAVE BOOKED TWICE
-----------------------
Do not assume one booking will automatically cancel. Contact support with each reservation reference so both records and related payments can be checked.

ROOM TYPE DIFFERS FROM CONFIRMATION
-----------------------------------
Keep the confirmation details and contact support. The team will verify the booked room type and coordinate with the property.
